Visual direction "Griglia Crouwel": the calendar grid stays visible as a construction armature, like a Wim Crouwel gridded type specimen. Palette: white ground #FFFFFF, faint blue construction grid hairlines #C9D8F2 visible behind everything at quarter-hour cells, one saturated plane of pure blue #0047FF used for today's column header and the primary button, black #111111 type. Type: a neutral small grotesque for labels, and the day numbers in the column headers constructed from grid cells like Crouwel's New Alphabet (only the date numerals, small enough to stay sober). Events are flat rectangles snapped exactly to grid cells, filled in account colours at low saturation (blue, green, amber) with black text. Signature move: today's date "7" built monumentally from grid cells in the header area of the Wednesday column, and the current time 14:20 marked by one blue cell row across the week.
