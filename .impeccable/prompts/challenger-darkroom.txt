Visual direction "Fogli di esposizione": a darkroom exposure record. Palette: neutral grey zone ramp only (#F2F2F0 ground, #BDBDB8, #5A5A57, #1C1C1B), plus one glowing magenta #D6267A used only to mark scheduling conflicts. Type: tracked condensed caps for labels, event times in tabular mono numerals. Each account is a translucent sheet: events are semi-transparent grey sheets with a thin outline, accounts distinguished by hatch angle and density rather than colour (local solid, Google 45-degree hatch, Microsoft 135-degree hatch). Signature move: where the Wednesday 14:00 and 14:30 events from two accounts overlap, the two hatches cross at ninety degrees and a magenta outline plus a small label "CONFLITTO" marks the double booking. The sidebar shows each account as a small hatched swatch.
