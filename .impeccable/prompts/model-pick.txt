Visual direction "Orario ferroviario": the week set like a Swiss railway timetable and departure board (SBB/FFS). Palette: pure white ground #FFFFFF, black #111111 text and rules, a single signal red #EB0000 for the present, dark grey #2B2B2B secondary. Type: a strict neutral grotesque like a timetable, hours in large bold tabular numerals in the left column, events set as timetable rows in precise columns (time, title, calendar shown as a small pictogram-like square in the account colour: blue, green, amber at low saturation). Thin black rules, no rounded cards, rectangles with square corners. Signature move: the present shown as a station-clock red second hand: a thin red line across the grid at 14:20 ending in a red disc on the hour axis, like Hans Hilfiker's clock hand. Sidebar is white with black type and account squares.
